  • Pausing a membership requires written notice 14 days prior to renewal date. No exceptions. Email theteam@floatarete.com.

  • Memberships may be paused for a minimum of 30 days and a maximum of 2 months.
  • At the end of the paused duration, your dues will continue with your next scheduled auto renewal date.
  • Memberships must renew one cycle before cancelling.
  • You will still have access to your credits while your membership is paused but not to other member benefits or perks.
