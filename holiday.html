Good to know.

Where do I redeem the retail offer?

In person, at the studio: 213 E. Braxton Foushee St in Carrboro. Bring the printed holiday offer card when you shop, and we take 25% off retail items. The offer does not apply to gift cards, services or memberships, and there is no online code.

Can I buy a gift card online?

Yes. Choose $50, $100, $150, $250 or your own amount. It is sent by email right away and is good for one year from purchase. See gift card terms.

Can I get a printed gift card at the studio?

Yes. Stop by and we will make a printed gift card in an envelope, ready to hand over.

What if I don’t know which treatment to choose?

Give a gift card for a dollar amount. It works for any service at Areté: float, infrared sauna, contrast therapy, massage, red light therapy and halotherapy. They choose later, online or at the front desk. Or call us at 919-636-9899 and we will help you pick.

How do I book a visit for myself?

Tap “Book an experience” below to pick a service and time. You need a credit or debit card to hold the appointment. See our booking and cancellation terms.
